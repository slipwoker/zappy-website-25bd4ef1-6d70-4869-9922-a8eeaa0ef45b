Everything You Need to Know About BrightDrop

From payment processing platforms to custom domains — we've answered the most common questions so you can launch with confidence.

01

What makes BrightDrop different from other digital stores?

BrightDrop is built from the ground up as a high-energy, creator-first digital storefront. Unlike generic platforms, we combine a striking visual identity with a complete launch toolkit — instant digital delivery, integrated payment processing services, custom domain support, and hosting guidance all in one place. You get a store that looks exclusive and works flawlessly, without cobbling together a dozen separate tools.

02

Can I sell digital products directly through BrightDrop?

Absolutely. BrightDrop is purpose-built for selling digital goods — templates, design assets, software, courses, e-books, presets, and more. Each product listing supports instant delivery upon purchase, so your customers receive their files immediately after checkout. No manual fulfillment, no delays. Just a clean, fast buying experience that keeps customers coming back.

03

How does payment setup work — and which payment processing platforms are supported?

Getting paid is straightforward. BrightDrop integrates with leading online payment processing companies including Stripe and PayPal, giving you access to credit card payment processing services right out of the box. Setup takes minutes — connect your account, set your prices, and you're ready to accept payments globally. We handle the technical complexity so you can focus on creating and selling.

04

Can I use my own custom domain with BrightDrop?

Yes — your brand, your domain. BrightDrop fully supports custom domain mapping, so your store can live at yourbrand.com instead of a generic subdomain. You can connect a domain you already own or register a new one. We walk you through every DNS step with clear guidance, making the process fast even if you've never set up a domain before. A custom domain builds trust and makes your store feel truly professional.

05

Does BrightDrop include hosting, or do I need to arrange that separately?

Hosting is fully included. Your BrightDrop store is hosted on fast, secure infrastructure — no separate hosting plan needed. We take care of uptime, performance, SSL certificates, and security so your store stays online and loads quickly for every customer. If you have questions about advanced hosting configurations or need guidance on migrating an existing store, our support team is ready to help at every step.
